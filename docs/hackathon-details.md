## 2. The challenge

- Global online hackathon. No set problem statements, no tracks.
- **The one fundamental rule: the project must meaningfully use both PayPal and AI.**
- "PayPal" includes all its services: Venmo, Braintree, Xoom, Honey, Zettle, Paidy, Hyperwallet and others.
- Individual or team. New project, or an existing one significantly updated during the submission period.
- More than one submission is allowed if each is substantially different.

## 3. Eligibility (rules §3)

Open to individuals at the age of majority, teams and organisations. Not open to residents of Brazil, Quebec,
Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea or other OFAC-sanctioned places; to employees or families of PayPal,
Devpost or anyone running the hackathon; or to any judge or a judge's employer. **[Sara]** An individual in
India is eligible.

## 4. Project requirements (rules §4)

Build or update an application that integrates the **PayPal developer platform (free sandbox)** with an AI
tool, model or platform of your choice. The project must:

1. Meaningfully integrate at least one PayPal technology, API, SDK, product or developer capability.
2. Meaningfully incorporate AI.
3. Be a working prototype or proof of concept that **installs and runs consistently and works as shown in the
   video and description**.
4. Be documented well enough for judges to understand and evaluate it.
5. Use third-party SDKs, APIs and data only as their licences allow.

Any AI tool is allowed, as long as PayPal integration is central. Sponsor tools are optional.

## 5. What to submit

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Text description | The project's features and functionality |
| Functional demo | Judges must be able to run or use a working build: **either** complete setup/run instructions in the repo **or** a hosted demo URL. Mockups, static prototypes and non-working demos do not count. |
| Testing access | Free and unrestricted for judges **until the judging period ends (15 Dec)**. Include any test credentials, API keys or sandbox logins needed, or clear instructions for getting them. |
| Tools | Which AI models/services were used, and how |
| Code repository | Public, on GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket. All source, assets and run instructions. **Open-source licence file, detectable and visible in the repo's About section.** |
| Demo video | **Under 3 minutes.** Shows the project working on the device it was built for. **Public on YouTube**, link on the form. No third-party trademarks or copyrighted music/material without permission. |
| Language | English (or English translations of everything) |

## 6. How judging works (rules §6)

- **Stage one, pass/fail:** does the project reasonably fit the theme and **reasonably apply the required
  PayPal APIs/SDKs**?
- **Stage two, five criteria, equally weighted:**

| # | Criterion | The judges' question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Technological implementation | How thoroughly and skilfully does it use the PayPal Developer Platform and AI? Genuine effort, working, non-trivial? |
| 2 | Design | A complete, coherent product experience, not just a technical proof of concept? |
| 3 | Potential impact | A credible, specific case for a real problem and a real audience, and does the demo show it being solved? |
| 4 | Innovation / idea | How creative and novel, and how does it differ from existing concepts? |
| 5 | Presentation | Does the video show it working end to end? Does the pitch say what problem, for whom, and why it matters? Easy to follow? |

- **Ties** are broken by criterion 1 first, then 2, and so on.
- Judging may use expert panels, peer review, **automated AI-driven analysis**, or a mix. Judges need not run
  the project and may judge on the description, images and video alone.

**Honourable mentions ($5,000 each, one winner each):** Most Creative · Most Impactful · Best Demo Delivery ·

payment within 60 days of the forms. Winners pay their own wire/exchange fees and taxes.

## 8. Ownership and rights (rules §4, §7, §10)

- The work must be original and owned by the entrant. Open-source components are fine if their licences are
  followed and the project builds on top of them.
- The entrant keeps the IP. PayPal gets a non-exclusive licence to use the entry for judging, and PayPal and
  Devpost may promote it (including name, likeness and voice) for three years.
- Not allowed: projects built with funding, contracts or commercial licences from PayPal or Devpost.
